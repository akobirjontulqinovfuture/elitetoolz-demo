Questions, answered

FAQ

How fast is my order delivered?

Instantly. As soon as your payment confirms (on-chain for USDT, or via Stripe for cards), our system reserves stock and releases your credentials automatically — usually within seconds.

Are these the same accounts as the Telegram bot?

Yes. The web store and ELITEXBOT share the same catalogue and live inventory, so stock and prices stay in lockstep wherever you buy.

What payment methods do you accept?

International credit and debit cards via Stripe, plus USDT on BEP-20 (Binance Pay compatible). Crypto orders show a QR code and exact amount, then verify automatically.

What if an account stops working?

Every product carries a replacement warranty. If credentials fail inside the warranty window, you get a free replacement or the amount back as store balance.

What if I need help?

Our support team runs 24/7 — the same team behind the Telegram bot. Reach out any time and we'll sort it out.
